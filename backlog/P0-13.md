## Plan

Filed on the user's word (2026-10-28), after EXPR-02 (PR #6).

**The gap.** P0-11 made `tools/checkers.lock` and `tools/mutants.expected` data that a change may
rewrite, judged by the gate's `checkers` and `mutants` rules, so that checker edits (Ring 1)
need no key. `tools/witnesses.expected` was left as gate code. EXPR-02 added a checker
(`bcheckF`, the fuelled body of `bcheck`), so the registry had to change. On GitHub the gate then
failed on `ring0` and also on `ci`: master's registry did not list the new checker, so
`witnesses.sh --check`, the registry witness, `check-claims` and `guard-selftest` all failed
under master's tools. The change was sound, and only the key could merge it.

**The rule.** The registry may grow and may change a checker's example witnesses. It may never
lose a checker or a witness.

1. `tools/witnesses.expected` joins the gate's data files (`DATA_FILES` and the `ring0`
   classifier's `DATA` list in `tools/gate/base-gate.sh`), like `tools/axioms.expected`.
2. A new gate rule, `registry`, compares BASE's registry with the PR's:
   - every checker in BASE is still in the PR (a checker removed or renamed needs the key);
   - every checker in the PR has at least 1 accepted and 1 refused witness (the counts in
     columns 2 and 3);
   - no checker's accepted or refused count goes down;
   - the summary line reports 0 missing.
3. `ci` keeps `witnesses.sh --check`, so the PR's registry must be current, computed from the
   PR's own environment. The gate rule judges how it moved from BASE.
4. Witnesses (`tools/gate/base-gate-witness.sh`, quick cases): adding a checker row with 1/1
   is accepted; dropping a row is refused; a checker's refused count going to 0 is refused;
   lowering an accepted count is refused.
5. Docs: the base-gate header and the README's frozen-statements paragraph list the registry
   among the data a change may update.

Out of scope: the two witnesses that pinned counts (mutants 318, warden drift 0/1) are already
fixed in EXPR-02. Once this lands, a change like EXPR-02 passes the gate without the key,
because its only other Ring 0 files were those two scripts.
