from S-435 (`bench/s435.tsv`): `if_neg` 585, `if_pos` 537, `if_true` 13, `dif_pos` 9,
`if_false` 6, `dif_neg` 5, `Int.ofNat_nonneg` 1, in conf-typing and transport-calculus.

1. Rename each to the replacement Lean names in the warning (`ite_eq_right`, `ite_eq_left`,
   `ite_true`, `dite_eq_left`, `ite_false`, `dite_eq_right`, `Int.natCast_nonneg`), module by
   module, building after each.

3. Do not touch the 58 unused-simp-argument or 6 unused-variable warnings; they predate 4.35.
