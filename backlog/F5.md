## Plan

From [FOUNDATION-2026-10-29](../research/2026-10/FOUNDATION-2026-10-29.md), item F5. Ring 1, parallel across agents once F2 and F3 are stable; rows taken in order of severity.
On branch `f5/map` (from master, after the foundation merge #17).

**Every isolation row names its attacker** (the user's decision of 2026-11-02). `Cert.relView` stays
L's isolation claim, for an observer that can measure side channels: it refuses a program whose
timing, addresses, cost or path depend on the other tenant's data, so that tenant's code must be
constant-time in its data. A row may use `Cert.hyper` over the observer's events only if it states
that the observer cannot see side channels. Leakage partitioned by tenant was offered and not chosen.

**The map, row by row, against what is proved on an L language today** (2026-11-02). "On L"
means a `Cert` instance on `ucmdLangT` or `ucmdLangS` with an adequacy theorem; everything else
is proved, if at all, over a toy semantics that is not L.

**Kill rule check** (3 misfits of the same kind): the three misfits are of three different kinds
(quantitative, concurrency, assumption), so the rule does not fire. Rows 3 and 4 are the
evidence to watch: both want `rel` relative to a different equivalence, and if a third row wants
the same, `Lang`'s single `lowIn` is the thing to change before F6 locks more.

**Steps.**
1. **The map says what is on L.** `VULN-COVERAGE.md` gains an "on L" column with the theorem
   names above, and a correction of record for each row whose "IN" was a toy-language claim; a
   check-claims guard keeps every named L theorem in the axiom manifest.
2. **Rows 3 and 4 onto L**, the two that test `Lang`'s single `lowIn`: a taint-indexed and a
   release-indexed instance of `ucmdLangS`, and `pcheck`'s and `SecTyD`'s soundness as `rel`
   on them (or a recorded misfit).
3. Rows 6 (leaks), 7b, 9, 14 in order of severity; 12's component seams with the kernel.
4. Unused `Ev`/`Lk` constructors: `Ev.release` (row 4), `Ev.ext` is produced (faults),
   `Lk.vtime` and `Lk.ext` are produced by nothing: produced by a row's language or removed.
   `Ev.release` is reserved for REL-01 (computed release), not removed, by the user's decision of
   2026-11-02.
