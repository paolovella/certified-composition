## Plan

From [`USABLE-L`](../research/2026-11/USABLE-L.md) §5, item 3. `addw` and `sllw` are a later optimisation: the reading masks with `& 0xffffffff`, which needs `CAP-01`'s large literals. Whether `uint32_t` becomes a type in L is left open (`USABLE-L` §6).

Three parts, riskiest last, since part 3 is the one with a kill rule:
1. **The machine.** `slli rd rs k` (I-type, funct3 1, the top six bits 0) and `sll rd rs1 rs2` (R-type, funct3 1, the shift `rs2 & 63`) in `Slice/Rv.lean`, with `W64.shl`. Compared with QEMU in `rv_diff.py`: amounts 0, 1, 31, 32 and 63, bits shifted out at the top, and `sll` reading only the low six bits of `rs2`. Broken models: an amount not masked, a shift right, an amount one short, and a decode that accepts `funct7 ≠ 0`.
2. **The language.** `Expr.shl a k`, `k < 64`, lowered to one `slli`, through the slots pass and the lowering (`genE_exec`, `mE`, `okE`), the budget and `highE`. The C front end reads `e << k` for a literal `k`, at `>>`'s level. Short shifts change no existing image. Witnesses: `shl 63` accepted and `shl 64` refused; `1 << 63` evaluates to `2^63`, `3 << 63` to `2^63`. Mutants in `slice2_low_mut.py` and `cfront_mut.py`.
3. **The `uint32_t` reading.** A note, `research/2026-11/W32-01-READING.md`: a `uint32_t` value is a word whose top 32 bits are zero, and each `+`, `-` and `<<` on it is followed by `& 0xffffffff`; `^ | & >>` keep the top bits zero, so they need no mask. Tested against clang: `ROTL32(x, k)` at every `k` from 1 to 31 and `+`, `-` at the wrap points, compiled by L and by clang, run under QEMU, outputs equal. Vacuity: the same programs without the mask disagree with clang.

The front end does not read `uint32_t` declarations in this item; the reading is applied by hand in the test programs. `CPP-01` brings the declarations, macros and helpers HChaCha20's text uses.
