# Step 6's fourth CI run was cut off when the VM went down

**What happened.** `tools/run-job.sh rc01-step6-ci4 -- bash tools/ci.sh` started at
15:15:45 on HEAD `9dc11bf`, with RC-01 step 6 uncommitted in the tree. At 15:35:44 the WSL
VM stopped: that is the last line in the journal of boot -1, and boot 0 starts at 15:38:11.
The job was in the rule-mutants step. Its log stops there, and no `.end` was written, because
nothing in the VM survived to write it.

**Not the cause.** The previous boot's journal has no OOM kill on 2026-11-08 (its last ones are
on 2026-11-04, in other projects' containers). Memory caps on our children
(`cap-long-running-children`) were not what failed.

**What it cost.** One CI run. The uncommitted step 6 work was on disk and is intact.

**Resolution.** `rc01-step6-ci4.end` is written by hand with `exit: killed` and a note, as for
`skernel-kernel.end` (I-0002). CI is run again on the quiet machine.

**What would catch it.** render-state already reports the open job, which is how this was
found. A wrapper cannot write a receipt when its host stops, so no guard is added.
